## Overview

An AR interactive product (Little Blue Dragon) developed for iOS combining 3D assets, animation and interactive storytelling. The project included mixed in-house and external resources to deliver an engaging mobile AR experience.

## My role

IT Project (Product) Manager — Sep 2018 — Sep 2021

I organized and managed a team of developers, 3D designers, and animators, engaged external contractors as needed, and led the product to launch on the App Store.

## Challenges

- Coordinating art, 3D modeling, and engineering to achieve a consistent interactive experience.
- Managing cross-discipline dependencies while keeping a user-centric product schedule.
- Optimizing performance on target mobile devices.

## Architecture & stack (high level)

- Platform: iOS (ARKit)
- Frontend: native iOS app with 3D assets, animations and interactive scenes
- Assets: 3D models, textures, animation timelines and audio
- Delivery: App Store release and iterative updates based on user feedback

## Solution & approach

- Formed a mixed team combining in-house roles and trusted external specialists for 3D and animation.
- Implemented a sprint cadence that prioritized playable milestones for user testing.
- Focused on early performance profiling to ensure fluid AR experience on supported iOS devices.

## Outcomes

- Successful App Store launch with positive feedback and ratings.
- Improved product quality through iterative user tests and performance tuning.
