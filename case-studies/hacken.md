## Overview

Worked as IT Project Manager on mobile security and crypto-related products within the Hacken ecosystem. The engagement focused on maintenance, feature development, and launching new mobile services.

## My role

IT Project Manager — Jan 2021 — May 2022

I coordinated development and release cycles for mobile apps, balanced maintenance with new feature development, and ensured product stability across platforms.

## Projects & links

- HakenAI (crypto wallet) — maintenance & feature development
- hPass — password manager (mobile)
- hVPN — VPN service for mobile and desktop

(App Store references are available in resume / projects page.)

## Challenges

- Maintaining security-sensitive mobile apps that handle user secrets and financial data.
- Coordinating cross-platform mobile development (iOS & Android) and desktop components for hVPN.
- Ensuring timely releases with rigorous QA and security reviews.

## Architecture & stack (high level)

- Mobile: native or cross-platform mobile stacks (iOS/Android builds) with backend services for auth and synchronization.
- Backend: secure API services for account management, encryption handling and telemetry.
- Distribution: App Store deployments and staged rollouts.

## Solution & approach

- Prioritized security and stability: clear release gates, automated tests, and security checklists.
- Managed parallel maintenance and new feature tracks with clear branching and CI pipelines.
- Collaborated closely with security engineers and QA to ensure compliance and safe rollouts.

## Outcomes

- Successful maintenance and ongoing development of security-focused mobile apps.
- Launched hPass and hVPN services to the company ecosystem with managed releases.
