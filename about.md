I build practical AI-powered products and software prototypes at the intersection of product thinking, software engineering, and AI systems architecture. My focus is on turning ideas into working applications — especially where LLMs, AI agents, retrieval, memory, and structured workflows can create useful product experiences.

I bring 15+ years of experience across software delivery, product development, technical project management, and engineering leadership. Today, I combine that product and delivery background with hands-on work in applied AI, rapid prototyping, and modern software architecture.

My approach is deliberately practical: understand the problem, identify where AI actually adds value, design the smallest useful system, build a working prototype, evaluate it, and iterate toward an MVP.

### What I care about
- Practical AI applications that solve real problems
- Clear architecture and maintainable systems
- AI agents, tool use, retrieval, memory, and structured state
- Strong product thinking and useful user experiences

### My approach
I start with the product problem rather than the technology. From there, I define the smallest system that can validate the idea, make technical and architectural trade-offs explicit, and build a working prototype quickly. I prefer controlled AI workflows over unnecessary complexity, with clear boundaries between application logic, models, data, retrieval, and agent behavior.

### Core interests
- Applied AI and Generative AI products
- AI agents and agentic workflows
- RAG, retrieval, memory, and contextual AI
- AI application architecture and API integration
- Rapid prototyping and MVP development
